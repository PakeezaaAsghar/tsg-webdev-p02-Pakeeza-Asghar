Our Story

Palmar was founded on a simple passion for clear coastal flavors and relaxed seaside luxury. We source our seafood directly from local artisan fisheries every morning, combining traditional preparation techniques with contemporary tropical mixology to bring an authentic, unhurried coastal paradise directly to your table.
